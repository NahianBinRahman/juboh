## 2. Approved Standard Corporate Copy

### 2.1 Hero Tagline & Descriptor
> **Heading:** Building an Exceptional Future, Together.  
> **Subcopy:** JUBOH Companies International is a privately held enterprise advancing real estate development, business strategy, government contracting, strategic investments, executive education, and community impact across the United States and select international markets.

### 2.2 Enterprise Introduction
> JUBOH Companies International operates across disciplined, complementary business sectors—uniting real estate development, capital advisory, public sector contracting, and civic initiatives into one integrated enterprise. Built upon operational rigor and institutional trust, the organization delivers lasting value for institutional partners, municipalities, and communities nationwide.

### 2.3 Pillar Descriptions (PDF Grounded)
- **Real Estate Development:** Master-development, commercial, residential, and institutional projects with a focus on enduring physical presence and community advancement.
- **Business Strategy & Capital Advisory:** Institutional advisory practice providing capital strategy, development advisory, and strategic advisory for corporate and civic partners.
- **Government Contracting:** Public-sector program execution, municipal and federal contracting capabilities, compliance, and institutional project delivery.
- **Executive Education:** Distinct leadership, executive training, private advisory, and cohort-based educational programs (separated from core corporate capabilities).
- **Foundation & Community Impact:** Philanthropic mission, community partnerships, educational empowerment, and civic investment initiatives.

## 3. Explicitly Prohibited Claims & Anti-Patterns (PDF & Policy Guardrails)

### 3.1 Quantitative & Metric Claims (DO NOT USE WITHOUT VERIFIED WRITTEN CLIENT PROOF)
- **DO NOT CLAIM:** `$1B+ Projects Supported`, `250+ Homes Delivered`, `20+ Markets Across the U.S.`, `100% Commitment to Community Impact`, or any specific dollar valuation, square footage, or unit count seen on the reference collage.
- **DO NOT CLAIM:** Specific number of years in business (e.g., "Over 25 years"), employee counts, contract volume, or historical IRR/returns.

### 3.2 Prohibited Conceptual & AI-Style Language (PDF p. 4, 6, 10)
- **Banned Terms:** *Generational architecture, wealth deployment ecosystem, institutional velocity multipliers, blueprint pillars, wealth frameworks, legacy multipliers, deployment flow, bulletproof, guaranteed outcomes, turnkey empire.*
- **Banned Presentation:** Generic multi-card SaaS grids, floating badges/pills, neon gradients, glassmorphism, animated numeric counters, fake direct-response coaching funnels, crossed-out pricing, or "50% grant" discount badges.

### 3.3 Unverified Entities & Case Studies
- **Subsidiaries:** Do NOT present unverified corporate entities (e.g. "JUBOH Capital", "JUBOH Construction", "JUBOH Development LLC") as distinct legal corporations unless legally validated. Refer to them as "Operating Disciplines" or "Enterprise Areas".
- **Fictional Projects:** Do NOT invent project names, mock municipal awards, or staged case studies (e.g., "Atlanta Mixed-Use", "Capitol Federal Complex") as verified JUBOH portfolio completions.
- **Unverified Leadership Identity:** Do NOT assign a name, title, bio, or credentials to the gentleman in `assets/leadership-portrait-unverified.jpg` until officially confirmed in writing by the client. Treat the file as an editorial photographic asset.
